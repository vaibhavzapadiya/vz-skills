## Research discipline

For each meaningful claim:
- record the exact source and date;
- separate observed facts from interpretation;
- verify repository license before adapting content;
- do not copy large sections of source text;
- document uncertainty and stale/ambiguous documentation;
- update this register when implementation depends on a platform detail.
